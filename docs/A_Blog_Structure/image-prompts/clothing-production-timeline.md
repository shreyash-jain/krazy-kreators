**1 — Hero (abstract/conceptual, 16:9)**
> A long paper production calendar pinned across a studio wall in late afternoon, ruled into weeks and marked up in pencil with arrows and circled dates. Shot on a Canon EOS-1V, 35mm prime, Kodak Portra 400, low raking window light, natural grain, foreground softly out of focus falling back to a sharp wall. Warm neutral grade. Shot on a Nikon F100, 28mm prime, Kodak Portra 400, available light only, slight handheld tilt, visible grain, cavernous and lived-in. Warm neutral grade. Small "KK" watermark bottom-right. Not a render, not CGI, no faces, no brand marks, no sewing-machine close-up.

**3 — Phase 2 (single garment / editorial, 1:1)**

Shot on a Contax G2, 45mm prime, Kodak Portra 400, shallow depth of field falling off toward the right, natural grain. Warm neutral grade. Small "KK" watermark bottom-right. Not a render, not CGI, no faces, no logos, no flat-lay.

**4 — Phase 4 (macro detail, 1:1)**
