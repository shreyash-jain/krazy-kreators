## 6 — `no-moq-clothing-manufacturers-moq-explained.jpg` (MOQ explainer)

- **Slot & purpose:** Section 2 ("Why minimums exist at all"). Explains, in one frame, that a MOQ is a stack of upstream minimums — not a factory's negotiating position.
- **Register:** Designed infographic (the one non-photo in the set).
- **The content it must carry** — six constraint rows feeding one result:

| # | Constraint | The minimum it imposes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mill dye lot | One batch dyed together; setup costs the same for 50 kg or 500 |
| 2 | Fabric roll | Cloth is sold by the roll per color, not by the metre |
| 3 | Pattern + grading | Paid once per style, whatever the quantity |
| 4 | Cutting lay + marker | A lay is only efficient above a certain depth of plies |
| 5 | Sewing line setup | Retooling a line costs the same for 30 pieces or 3,000 |
| 6 | Customs entry | Flat fees per shipment, spread over however many units ship |
| → | **Result** | **The MOQ the factory quotes you — set per style AND per color** |
