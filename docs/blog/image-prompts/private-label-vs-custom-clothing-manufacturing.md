### 1. Hero — `private-label-vs-custom-hero.jpg` · 1:1

**Purpose:** the article's thesis in one frame — the same garment as a finished thing you buy, and as a
thing you build. **Composition:** overhead-adjacent three-quarter view across a design studio table,
split down the middle: left, a plain finished cotton tee folded neatly; right, the same garment
disassembled into its inputs — a manila pattern piece, a fabric header, a printed spec sheet, a loose
neck tape. Two hands resting still on the table between the halves. **Lighting:** broad soft north
window light from the left, gentle falloff to the right side of the frame. **Mood:** a decision held
open, unhurried, quietly consequential.

### 4. Ownership — `private-label-vs-custom-macro.jpg` · 1:1

**Purpose:** the asset you walk away with from custom production — a graded pattern.
**Composition:** extreme macro along the edge of a manila pattern card: a punched drill hole, two cut
notches, and pencilled grading lines stepping outward across the board in a fan; the card's cut edge
slightly furred with use. A folded edge of cotton jersey sits beneath, well out of focus. **Lighting:**
low raking light from the right so the pencil lines and the card's tooth catch and the notches throw
small hard shadows. **Mood:** archival, worked-on, quietly valuable.

> Shot on a Canon EOS-1V with a 100mm f/2.8 macro on Kodak Ektar 100. Extreme close-up along the edge of
> a manila pattern card: a punched drill hole, two cut notches, pencilled grading lines fanning outward,
> the cut edge furred from use. A folded edge of cotton jersey far out of focus beneath. Low raking
> light from the right, hard small shadows, visible paper tooth, shallow depth of field, natural grain.
