**Third revision. What was wrong with the previous two sets, so nobody reintroduces it:**
- **v1 was stock-lit** - golden-hour hero, a dust-lit god-ray in the closing frame, a staged "cold half-drunk coffee" prop. Those are the classic generated-image tells.
- **v2 overcorrected into sterile** - no people in any of the five and flat overcast light in four of them. An empty, evenly-lit room reads as AI just as strongly as a god-ray does. Emptiness is not realism.
- **v3 (below) puts people back in and varies the light.** Three of the five carry human presence as hands or a back - which MASTER_PROMPT explicitly permits - and the five lighting setups are genuinely different: mixed high-bay/clerestory, plain office overhead, soft window backlight, diffused macro light, and daylight-against-fluorescent at a dock.

**Rules applied to all five:**
- **No legible text, lettering or numbers.** Phrase it as "soft and unreadable" rather than "no text", so paperwork and cartons still carry realistic printed *texture* without asking the model to spell. This repo has shipped garbled AI text twice (id 64's hero calendar skipped Wk 39; id 59's tech-pack callout read "careolock").
- **Hands and backs only, never faces.** Human presence is what makes a working environment photograph read as real.
- **Visible wear is required** - scuffed floor paint, stray tape, creased paper, coffee rings.
- No real logos or brand marks; small "KK" watermark bottom-right; one cohesive warm-neutral grade across all five.
