### Option B — Direct & Educational

🧵 What is a Tech Pack? (And why you literally can't manufacture without one)

If you're a fashion designer moving into production, this is the ONE document you cannot skip.

A Tech Pack is your garment's blueprint. It includes:

📐 Technical Flat Sketches — front, back, side views with every seam and stitch line
📋 Bill of Materials — every fabric, button, zipper, thread, and label specified
📏 Grading Rules — exact measurements for every size, not just your sample size
🪡 Construction Details — SPI, hem types, wash treatments — zero room for guessing

Without it, factories make assumptions. Those assumptions cost you samples, time, and money.
